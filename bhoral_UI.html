Bhoral - Archive Website Design


Bhoral is a project focused on preserving and narrating Indian folklores in digital form, beginning with Assam.

Project details

  • Category:School Project
  • Year: 2022
  • Expertise: Ethnography, System Design, Participarory Design
  • Area:Digital Humanities, Archive, Folk Culture

Bhoral is a project focused on preserving and narrating folklores in digital form, beginning with Assam. The goal was to create a lightweight open digital repository, accessible across devices and visually compelling to the modern audience.
